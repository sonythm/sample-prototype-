Agent orchestration behind a trust perimeter

A working prototype of one layered design applied across industries. Every request, from a person, a machine or a partner API, crosses the trust perimeter first. A central orchestrator plans the route, domain agents act only inside their scopes, machines and third-party systems are reached only through the integration layer, and every handoff carries a stateful, hash-chained envelope.
